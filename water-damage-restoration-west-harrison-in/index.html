West Harrison is a very small Dearborn County town, about 8 miles north of Guilford by straight line, and it sits directly against the Ohio border. If water is spreading through your house, call the 24/7 emergency line before you start mopping. This page covers the Indiana side, ZIP 47060.

The town across the street from Ohio

Wikipedia notes that West Harrison was laid out in 1813, the same year as its twin, Harrison, Ohio. State Street divides the two. The town is in Harrison Township, covers 0.23 square miles and had 325 residents in the 2020 census. Settlers found numerous mounds when they arrived.

For a water damage call, the border matters in one practical way. Give the full address and say Indiana, so the call is placed on the Indiana side. The listed elevation is 515 feet, low enough to sit near river level, but the practical risk here is plumbing and not river flooding.

When water is spreading in West Harrison

Small towns do not slow the clock. A supply line that fails at night will flood a floor by morning. Calls are taken around the clock, and the team offers same-day response when it can reach your location.

Close the main

Find the main valve, usually where the line enters the house, and turn it fully off.

Kill power to wet circuits

Only when the panel is dry and easy to reach. If not, wait for the utility.

Lift contents

Pull rugs, boxes and small furniture up and out. Photograph before and after.

Call before cleaning

Standing water is easier to remove before it soaks into carpet pad and subfloor.

The leaks we expect most in West Harrison

In a small town, the calls we expect most start inside the house. Water heaters, dishwashers, washers and supply lines fail more often than anything else. Each leaves a different pattern of damage.

Water heater failures

Tank leaks flood utility rooms and basements quickly. See water heater leak cleanup.

Appliance leaks

Washers, dishwashers and fridge lines drip into floors. See appliance leak water damage.

Split supply lines

A burst line behind a wall sends water through several rooms. See burst pipe water damage.

Overnight dispatch

West Harrison calls are dispatched around the clock, nights and holidays included. Scope and price are laid out before work starts.

Water that comes from a clean supply line is the easiest case, and acting within hours keeps it that way. Wet carpet and pad, baseboards and the bottom few inches of drywall are the usual casualties. Fast extraction and drying can save a lot of that, while a slow response means removal. Photograph the damage and keep receipts for any emergency purchases, since your insurer may ask for them.

A small town also means a short list of familiar roads. Tell us the cross street, any gate or access note and whether pets are in the house, so the crew can plan the visit before they arrive.
