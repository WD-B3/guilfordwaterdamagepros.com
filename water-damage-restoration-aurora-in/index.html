Aurora sits on the Ohio River about 9 miles south of Guilford by straight line. Many of its buildings are old, and the city has a National Register downtown district, so water damage here often involves aged plumbing, plaster and masonry. If water is spreading right now, call the 24/7 emergency line before you start cleanup.

Older and historic buildings in Aurora

Downtown Aurora has a National Register historic district. It includes Aurora City Hall, the Aurora Public Library, the George Street Bridge and the Leive, Parks and Stapp Opera House. Hillforest, the mansion finished in 1855 for Thomas Gaff, became a National Historic Landmark in 1992. Buildings of that age were not built with modern moisture control in mind.

Age changes the repair plan. Plaster and old trim dry differently from modern drywall, and cast iron or galvanized lines can fail at joints. Moisture readings show where the wet area really ends. Valuable woodwork is protected first, and cutting is kept to the minimum that the readings allow.

When water is spreading in Aurora

Overnight leaks and storm water are the usual 2 a.m. calls. Same-day response depends on who is free for your address, and the line stays open outside normal hours. Until help is on the way, work through these steps.

Find the shutoff

Close the main valve or the fixture valve, and tell us if it will not turn.

Power down only if safe

If the breaker panel is dry and reachable, switch off wet circuits. If not, step back.

Save the valuable items

Pull framed pictures, papers and electronics clear of the wet area, and photograph first.

Avoid dirty water

Keep children and pets out of water from drains, toilets or the yard.

Two elevations, two kinds of water

Aurora's listed elevation is 515 feet, near the river, and parts of the city climb the hillside above. Low streets deal with water coming from outside, so flood damage restoration and water extraction come first there. Higher streets see roof leaks, plumbing failures and runoff. The first question on the call is where the water is coming from.

Common questions

Is emergency water removal available in Aurora?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes calls at any hour, and a crew can often come the same day, depending on availability.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Aurora?

Aurora has one ZIP code, 47001. It covers the river-level streets, the downtown historic district and the hillside neighborhoods.

Can plaster walls be saved after a leak?

Often, yes, if they are dried slowly and the readings reach the dry standard. Cracked or crumbling plaster is replaced.

Does flood water from the river get handled differently?

Yes. Outside water is treated as contaminated, so porous materials are removed and surfaces are cleaned before drying.
