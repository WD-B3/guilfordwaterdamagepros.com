Water damage comes from many sources, and the right response depends on the cause. This page groups every service by what went wrong, from plumbing failures to storm water to wet floors. Each page explains the situation, the steps and the questions to ask. Start with the group that matches your problem.

If water is still spreading, do not browse. Shut off the source if you can, then call the 24/7 emergency line.

Pipes and plumbing

Burst Pipe Water Damage

A split supply line floods rooms fast. Stop the water, extract it and dry the walls and floors it reached.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage

Frozen lines split quietly and leak when they thaw. Cleanup, drying and what to do about the next freeze.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup

Tank failures flood utility rooms and basements. Extraction, drying and dealing with wet drywall and flooring.

Slab Leak Water Damage

Leaks beneath a concrete slab show up as warm floors or high bills. Locating, drying and repairing the damage.

Appliances and HVAC

Appliance Leak Water Damage

Washers, dishwashers and refrigerator lines drip into floors. Find the source and dry what it soaked.

AC Leak Water Damage

Clogged condensate lines overflow into ceilings and closets. Drying and preventing a repeat.

Kitchen Water Damage

Cabinets, counters and floors hold water in tight spaces. Dry in place or replace what swelled.

Weather and building envelope

Ceiling Water Damage Repair

Stains, sagging and roof leaks. Trace the water, dry the ceiling and repair what failed.

Storm Water Damage

Wind-driven rain and storm openings let water into the house. Protect, dry and repair the interior.

Flood Damage Cleanup

Outside water from creeks and runoff carries contamination. Safe removal, cleaning and drying.

Basement Water Damage

Pump-out, cleaning and drying for flooded or seeping basements and lower levels.

Crawl Space Water Damage

Water under the house goes unseen for weeks. Drying, insulation and moisture control.

Water removal and drying

Water Extraction

The first step on most jobs. Pumps and extractors remove standing water before it soaks in.

Structural Drying

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry framing, floors and wall cavities until readings reach the dry standard.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Contaminated water from drains and toilets. Containment, removal and disinfection.

Floors and walls

Hardwood Floor Water Damage

Cupping and buckling in wood floors. Controlled drying, sanding or replacement.

Carpet Water Damage

Extraction, drying and the carpet and pad decision, based on the water category.

Wall and Drywall Water Damage

Wicking, soft drywall and wet cavities. Dry in place or cut out and rebuild.

Commercial

Commercial Water Damage

Offices, shops and multi-unit properties. Planned around operations and downtime.

How to choose a service page

Match the page to the cause first, then to the damaged material. A pipe that burst in a kitchen touches both the burst pipe page and the kitchen page. Read the one that fits the source, then follow the link to the material page. The emergency steps are the same either way: stop the water, stay safe and call.

How every job runs

1

Call

Describe the water, where it came from and what it touched.

2

Extract

Standing water is removed first so it stops soaking in.

3

Dry

Equipment runs and moisture readings show progress.

4

Repair

Drywall, flooring and finishes are repaired, or the scope is documented for your insurer.
