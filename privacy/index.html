Privacy Policy and Call Recording Notice

This site has no forms and no accounts. The main information we receive is what you tell us on a phone call.

This policy explains what information Guilford Water Damage Pros receives when you visit this website or call the emergency line, and how it is used. It was last updated in 2026.

Calls may be recorded

Calls to the emergency line may be recorded and monitored for quality, training and to keep an accurate record of the job. By continuing a call after hearing this notice, you agree to the recording. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person at the start of the call.

Information you give us by phone

When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address, a description of the water damage and details about your insurance. We use this to respond to your request, plan the work, document the job and answer follow-up questions. We do not sell this information.

Information collected by this website

This site has no contact forms, accounts or comment sections. Our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, which is standard for web servers and is used for security and reliability.

Third-party services on this site

  • Google Fonts loads the typefaces used on the pages.
  • Google Maps embeds on some pages load map content from Google when the page is viewed.

These services may receive your IP address and browser information under their own privacy policies. We do not control how they handle that data.

How information is shared

We share information only as needed to do the work and handle your claim, such as with your insurer at your request, with trade partners working on your job or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it, subject to legal and record-keeping duties. Call the line and say that you have a privacy request.

Children

This site is meant for adult homeowners and businesses and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The updated version will appear on this page with a new date.

Questions about this policy?

Call the line and ask.
