Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Guilford, IN

Cabinets, countertops and the area under the sink hold water in places a mop cannot reach. Particleboard swells and delaminates fast, so quick drying makes a difference.

Kitchen water damage restoration is the drying and repair of cabinets, flooring, walls and countertops after a leak or spill in a kitchen. In Guilford, the usual sources are a leaking sink supply line, a dishwasher failure, a refrigerator water line or a drain under the sink. If water is running, close the valve under the sink or at the appliance and call the line.

Kitchens hold more water-sensitive materials than most rooms. Particleboard cabinet bases swell, laminate counters peel and wood floors cup. The faster the area dries, the more of it you can keep.

Why kitchens are different

Most kitchen cabinets are built from particleboard or MDF with a thin veneer. When water gets under the toe-kick or into the cabinet floor, the material swells and does not shrink back. Solid wood and plywood cabinets handle moisture better but can still stain and warp. Whether a cabinet can be saved often depends on how long it stayed wet.

Water also travels along the cabinet back and into the wall cavity behind. A leak at the sink supply can wet the wall for days before a stain shows. The wall and drywall page describes how that is handled.

Under-sink leaks

Supply lines, drains and the garbage disposal leak into the cabinet base. Many are slow, unseen leaks.

Dishwasher and refrigerator lines

Lines behind and beside appliances drip into the floor and toe-kicks. See appliance leak water damage.

Countertop seams and sinks

Failed caulk lets water into the cabinet top. Laminate counters swell at the edges.

Burst supply lines

A split pipe in a wall can flood a kitchen quickly. See burst pipe water damage.

What the restoration process looks like

1

Stop the water and empty the area

Valves are closed. Cabinet contents are removed so the base can be inspected.

2

Open and inspect

Toe-kicks are removed. Moisture readings show what the wall and floor hold.

3

Dry

Air movers and dehumidifiers run. Panel systems can dry hardwood under cabinets.

4

Repair or replace

Damaged cabinet bases, flooring and countertops are repaired or replaced.

Floors in kitchens vary, from tile to vinyl to hardwood. Water that went under the cabinets may not show until the floor lifts. If the floor is wood, the hardwood floor page explains cupping and recovery.

What to do first, and what not to do

  • Close the valve under the sink or at the appliance that is leaking.
  • Do not touch outlets, cords or appliances standing in water.
  • Do not run the dishwasher, disposal or other appliances until the area is checked.
  • Do not pull on a swollen cabinet base. Let a technician open it so the damage is mapped first.

Common questions

Can water-damaged kitchen cabinets be saved?

Plywood and solid wood often can, if dried quickly. Particleboard bases that swelled usually cannot.

How do I know if water got behind my cabinets?

Look for stains, soft spots and musty smells at the toe-kick and back wall. A moisture meter can confirm it.

Do you handle kitchen water emergencies at night or on weekends in Guilford?

A leak that is still running counts as an emergency at any hour. A cabinet found damp later can be booked for a regular visit.

Is mold a risk after a kitchen leak?

It can be if materials stay wet. Fast drying lowers the risk, and ongoing dampness should be checked.
