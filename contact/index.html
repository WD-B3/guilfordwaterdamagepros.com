Call Now

The fastest way to reach us is by phone. Call (812) 642-7696 at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays. Calls go to the emergency line, not a front desk, so describe the water and the address, and we will explain the next steps.

What to say on the call

  1. Your name and the address, including town and ZIP.
  2. Where the water came from and whether it is still running.
  3. Which rooms are wet and how deep the water is.
  4. Whether anyone is in danger or power is involved.
  5. Whether you plan to file an insurance claim.

While you wait

Shut the main valve if you can reach it safely. Stay out of water near outlets. Move valuables to dry areas and take photographs before you move things. If you smell gas or see sparking, leave the house and call 911.
