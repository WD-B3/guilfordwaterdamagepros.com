Bright is an unincorporated community in northeastern Dearborn County, about 4 miles northeast of Guilford in a straight line. Its eastern edge follows the Indiana-Ohio line. If water is spreading through a home here, call the emergency line first. Work starts with water extraction and continues with structural drying until readings come back to a normal level.

What Bright is

Bright is a Census-designated place with 5,814 people and 2,249 housing units in the 2020 Census, and 2.3 percent of those units were vacant. It covers about 12.6 square miles and reaches into Logan, Harrison and Miller townships. That works out to roughly 460 people per square mile, so homes are more spread out than in a town. The listed elevation is 938 feet.

Bright uses ZIP code 47025, the same ZIP code as Lawrenceburg, Greendale and Hidden Valley. A shared ZIP code is a mail route and does not mean the same city. Bright is also not part of Guilford. This page covers the Indiana side of the line only.

Flood maps and Bright

On November 9, 2026 we checked FEMA's current National Flood Hazard Layer by sampling about 9,400 points inside the Census boundary. About 98 percent fell in Zone X, FEMA's area of minimal flood hazard. About 2 percent fell in mapped Zone A, and a very small share fell in other mapped zones, which follow stream corridors. This is a rough area check and not a finding for any address. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified.

We found no documented flood event for Bright, so we do not describe it as flood-prone. Most water damage starts with ordinary causes such as plumbing, appliances, roofs and drains. If outside water is involved, see flood damage restoration.

Find the shutoff

Look for the valve under the fixture or at the main. A stuck valve is worth mentioning on the call.

Watch for electrical risk

Water near outlets, appliances or the panel is a reason to step outside and call the utility or 911, not a reason to flip breakers.

Record what you see

A few wide photos and close-ups of the source and the damage help later. Then move small valuables to a dry room.

Treat drain and outdoor water as unsafe

Sewage and yard runoff carry germs. Keep children and pets back, and do not try to dry it with household fans.

What to tell us when you call

Homes in Bright are not all on the same type of water or sewer service, and we have not verified which streets use which. Tell us if your home uses a septic system or a private well, because a septic backup and a clean supply-line leak are handled very differently. Give the street, the nearest crossroad and any gate or long driveway so the team can find you.

Insurance and records

Whether a loss is covered turns on how the water got in and on your policy's exact terms, so your insurer is the one to ask. A set of dated photos, readings and a written scope gives you something concrete to hand over. No one on this line can approve a claim or promise a payout. If you are near the state line, West Harrison is the closest other town page.

Common questions

Is Bright part of Guilford?

No. Bright is its own unincorporated community, roughly 4 miles northeast of Guilford, and one phone number answers for both.

Which ZIP code does Bright use?

47025. Several other communities use it as well, so the ZIP code alone does not say whether a home is in Bright.

Is Bright in a flood zone?

Most of it appears to be in Zone X on FEMA's current maps, with small mapped strips along streams. That says nothing certain about your address, so look it up in FEMA's Flood Map Service Center and ask your insurer.

Do you serve the Ohio side of the line?

This page is about the Indiana side. For an Ohio address, call and ask whether anyone is available there.

Will insurance pay for the cleanup?

That is up to your insurer and your policy. Photos and a written scope can describe the damage, but they do not decide coverage.
